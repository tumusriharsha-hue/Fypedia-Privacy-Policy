Fypedia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sriharsha Tumu ("Fypedia," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Fypedia mobile application and related services.

Fypedia is an educational application that helps users discover educational videos and reinforce learning through AI-generated questions.

By using Fypedia, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you use Fypedia, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication-related information.
  • Learning information: Selected topics, learning goals, quiz responses, learning statistics, session progress, and recently viewed topics.
  • Video information: YouTube video identifiers, URLs, titles, descriptions, available transcripts, and other video metadata needed to provide educational features.
  • Technical information: Information necessary to authenticate requests, operate backend services, troubleshoot errors, and maintain the security of Fypedia.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information for AI-generated learning questions.

3. Local Storage and Backend Processing

Fypedia uses Apple's Keychain to store authentication credentials securely on your device.

Fypedia also uses iOS UserDefaults to store certain appearance settings, profile cache information, learning statistics, recent topics, preferences, and session-related information locally.

Account and profile information may be stored or processed through InsForge, our backend infrastructure provider. Fypedia also uses server-side functions to retrieve educational video information, process transcripts, and generate learning questions.

Information stored locally on your device is not necessarily transmitted to our servers. Certain information may be transmitted when necessary to provide a requested feature.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Restore sessions and maintain account preferences.
  • Personalize educational topics and learning feeds.
  • Search for and display educational YouTube videos.
  • Retrieve and process video transcripts.
  • Generate educational questions using AI.
  • Display quiz results and learning progress.
  • Prevent duplicate requests and technical errors.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

5. Third-Party Services

Fypedia relies on the following third-party services to provide its functionality. Information processed by these providers may be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

InsForge

InsForge provides backend infrastructure for authentication, user profiles, account deletion, and server-side functions. Account identifiers, profile information, and information needed to fulfill backend requests may be processed by InsForge.

More information: InsForge.

YouTube and Google

Fypedia uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API and YouTube IFrame Player API, to discover and display educational YouTube videos.

When you use YouTube-powered features, YouTube and Google may receive information associated with requests and embedded video playback, such as device, network, and interaction information, according to their policies.

By using YouTube features within Fypedia, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

For information about Google's data practices, review the Google Privacy Policy .

Fypedia is an independent application and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

TranscriptAPI

Fypedia uses TranscriptAPI to retrieve transcripts associated with YouTube videos. Video identifiers, URLs, or other information necessary to retrieve transcripts may be transmitted to this provider.

Retrieved transcripts are used to support educational question generation. Transcript requests and processing may be subject to the provider's own data handling and retention practices.

More information: TranscriptAPI .

xAI Grok

Fypedia uses xAI's Grok API through server-side functions to generate educational quiz questions based on video transcripts and relevant learning context.

Video transcripts, video metadata, selected topics, and instructions needed to generate questions may be transmitted to xAI for processing.

We do not intentionally include user passwords or authentication credentials in AI requests. Information submitted to xAI may be processed and retained according to the provider's applicable terms and policies.

More information: xAI Privacy Policy .

6. Artificial Intelligence

Fypedia uses artificial intelligence to generate educational questions based on available video information and transcripts.

AI-generated questions and answers may contain errors or inaccuracies. Users should not rely on AI-generated content as a guaranteed source of factual accuracy.

Fypedia does not intentionally send authentication credentials to AI providers. Relevant learning context may be processed when needed to provide AI-powered functionality.

7. Advertising and Tracking

Fypedia does not currently include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising or tracking.

Embedded YouTube content and other third-party services may independently process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell users' personal information to advertisers.

8. Data Sharing

We share or transmit information to service providers when necessary to operate Fypedia's features, including authentication, video discovery, transcript retrieval, and question generation.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the security and integrity of our services.

We do not intentionally share personal account information with other Fypedia users.

9. Data Retention

We retain account and profile information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Fypedia's services, maintain user accounts, and fulfill applicable legal obligations.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until it is cleared through applicable application functionality or the app is removed, subject to iOS storage behavior.

Third-party service providers may retain requests, logs, and other processed information according to their own applicable retention policies.

We aim to limit unnecessary retention of information and remove personal information when it is no longer required for its intended purpose, subject to applicable legal obligations.

10. Security

Fypedia uses HTTPS for network communications and Apple's Keychain for local authentication credentials. API credentials used for production backend services are managed server-side.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Account Deletion

Users can initiate account deletion through the Settings section of Fypedia.

After successful server-side account deletion, Fypedia clears locally stored authentication credentials, cached profile information, learning statistics, recent topics, and account-specific session information.

Account deletion is intended to remove associated account and profile information maintained by Fypedia's backend, except information that must be retained for applicable legal, security, or regulatory obligations.

Deletion of information previously processed by independent third-party providers may be subject to their respective retention policies.

For assistance with account deletion or personal data requests, contact snaply.dev@gmail.com .

12. Children's Privacy

Fypedia is an educational application and may present content intended for a broad audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable children's privacy laws.

Where parental consent or other authorization is required by law, such authorization must be obtained before collecting personal information from a child.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information without the required authorization, please contact us at snaply.dev@gmail.com so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction, or deletion.

You can manage certain preferences within Fypedia and request account deletion through the application's Settings section.

For additional privacy requests, contact snaply.dev@gmail.com . We will review requests in accordance with applicable laws.

14. International Data Processing

Fypedia relies on third-party infrastructure and API providers. Information processed by these services may be transferred to or processed in countries outside your country of residence.

Such processing is subject to applicable legal requirements and the relevant providers' privacy and security practices.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Fypedia evolves or when our services, third-party providers, data practices, or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be made available through Fypedia's official Privacy Policy page. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
