Fypedia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. About This Policy

Fypedia is an educational application co-developed and managed by Sriharsha Tumu and Raghav Sridhar, and published by Ravichandra Tumu through an individual Apple Developer account.

This Privacy Policy explains how Fypedia ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Fypedia mobile application and related services.

Ravichandra Tumu is the publisher responsible for Fypedia's privacy practices. Sriharsha Tumu and Raghav Sridhar assist with developing, maintaining, and operating the application.

Fypedia helps users discover educational videos and reinforce learning through AI-generated questions.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you use Fypedia, we may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication-related information.
  • Learning information: Selected topics, learning goals, quiz responses, statistics, session progress, and recent topics.
  • Video information: YouTube video identifiers, URLs, titles, descriptions, available transcripts, and relevant video metadata.
  • Technical information: Information necessary to authenticate requests, operate backend services, troubleshoot errors, and maintain application security.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information for AI-generated learning questions.

3. Local Storage and Backend Processing

Fypedia uses Apple's Keychain to securely store authentication credentials on your device.

Fypedia uses iOS UserDefaults to store appearance settings, cached profile information, learning statistics, recent topics, preferences, and session-related information locally.

Account and profile information may be stored or processed using InsForge, our backend infrastructure provider.

Server-side functions are used to retrieve educational video information, process transcripts, and generate learning questions.

Information stored locally is not necessarily transmitted to our servers. Certain information may be transmitted when required to provide requested features.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage accounts.
  • Restore sessions and maintain preferences.
  • Personalize learning topics and feeds.
  • Search for and display educational videos.
  • Retrieve and process video transcripts.
  • Generate AI-powered educational questions.
  • Display quiz results and learning progress.
  • Prevent duplicate requests and technical errors.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

5. Third-Party Services

Fypedia relies on third-party services to provide its functionality. Information processed by these providers may be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

InsForge

InsForge provides backend infrastructure for authentication, user profiles, account deletion, and server-side functions.

Account identifiers, profile information, and information necessary to fulfill backend requests may be processed through InsForge.

More information: InsForge .

YouTube and Google

Fypedia uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API and YouTube IFrame Player API, to discover and display educational videos.

When you use YouTube-powered features, YouTube and Google may receive device, network, and interaction information according to their own policies.

Use of YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

For more information about Google's data practices, review the Google Privacy Policy .

Fypedia is an independent application and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

TranscriptAPI

Fypedia uses TranscriptAPI to retrieve available transcripts associated with YouTube videos.

Video identifiers, URLs, or other information required for transcript retrieval may be transmitted to TranscriptAPI.

Retrieved transcripts are processed to support educational question generation. TranscriptAPI may process or retain request information under its own applicable policies.

More information: TranscriptAPI .

xAI Grok

Fypedia uses xAI's Grok API through server-side functions to generate educational quiz questions based on video transcripts and learning context.

Transcript text, video metadata, selected topics, and instructions needed for question generation may be transmitted to xAI.

Authentication credentials and passwords are not intentionally included in AI requests. Information transmitted to xAI may be processed or retained according to its applicable terms.

More information: xAI Privacy Policy .

6. Artificial Intelligence

Fypedia uses AI to generate educational questions from available video information and transcripts.

AI-generated questions and explanations may contain errors or inaccuracies and should not be considered guaranteed factual information.

Relevant learning context may be processed when necessary to provide AI features.

7. Advertising and Tracking

Fypedia does not currently include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

We do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising or tracking.

Embedded YouTube content and other third-party services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell users' personal information to advertisers.

8. Data Sharing

We share or transmit information to service providers when necessary for authentication, video discovery, transcript retrieval, question generation, and other app features.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, valid legal requests, or necessary security obligations.

We do not intentionally share personal account information with other Fypedia users.

9. Data Retention

We retain account and profile information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Fypedia's services and fulfill applicable legal obligations.

Certain information is stored locally until it is cleared through applicable app functionality or device storage behavior.

Fypedia's production question-generation function processes transcript text temporarily. The reviewed implementation does not intentionally save raw transcripts in Fypedia's application database.

Independent providers, including TranscriptAPI and xAI, may retain requests, logs, or other processed information under their respective policies and contractual terms.

We aim to avoid unnecessary retention of information and remove personal data when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

10. Security

Fypedia uses HTTPS for network communication and Apple's Keychain for local authentication credentials.

Production API credentials are managed server-side rather than included in the production iOS client.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

No electronic transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

11. Account Deletion

Users can initiate account deletion through Fypedia's Settings section.

Following successful server-side deletion, Fypedia clears local authentication credentials, profile cache, learning statistics, recent topics, and account-specific session information.

Account deletion is intended to remove associated authentication and profile information maintained by Fypedia's backend, except information required for legal or legitimate security obligations.

Independent third-party providers may retain information under their own applicable retention policies.

For account deletion assistance, contact snaply.dev@gmail.com .

12. Children's Privacy

Fypedia is an educational application intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable children's privacy laws.

Where parental authorization is required by law, such authorization must be obtained before collecting a child's personal information.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided information without the necessary authorization may contact snaply.dev@gmail.com to request review or deletion.

13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

You can manage certain preferences and initiate account deletion within Fypedia's Settings section.

Additional requests can be sent to snaply.dev@gmail.com .

14. International Data Processing

Fypedia uses third-party infrastructure and API providers that may process information in countries outside your country of residence.

Such processing is subject to applicable legal requirements and provider privacy practices.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Fypedia's features, services, data practices, or legal obligations change.

Updates will be published on Fypedia's official Privacy Policy page with a revised update date.
