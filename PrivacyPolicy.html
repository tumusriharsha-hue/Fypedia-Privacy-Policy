Fypedia Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how [YOUR LEGAL NAME] (“Fypedia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Fypedia app and related services.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use Fypedia, we may handle your email address, display name, account identifier, authentication information, selected topics, learning goals, learning statistics, recent topics, quiz answers, and session progress.

How information is stored

Authentication credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. Some profile information, topic preferences, statistics, recent topics, and temporary session state are stored locally on your device. Account and profile information may also be processed by our backend provider.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, restore sessions, personalize learning topics, prepare learning feeds, generate video-grounded questions, show results, prevent duplicate requests, provide support, and maintain the service.

Third-party services

Fypedia uses InsForge for authentication and profile operations. It uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube’s embedded player to find and play videos. Video URLs, transcripts or video information, selected topics, and relevant learning context may be sent to transcript and question-generation providers to prepare questions. These providers process information under their own policies and terms.

Advertising and tracking

The current Fypedia client does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, an advertising identifier, or an analytics SDK. Update this section if those practices change.

Security

We use HTTPS for network requests and the iOS Keychain for local authentication credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Account deletion

You can request permanent account deletion from Settings. After successful server-side deletion, Fypedia clears local credentials, profile data, learning statistics, recent topics, and account-specific session state. Some information may be retained when required by law or legitimate security obligations.

Children

Fypedia is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, services, or data practices change. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to [SUPPORT_EMAIL].
